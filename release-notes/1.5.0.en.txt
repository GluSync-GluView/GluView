Bands and watches: the vibration of each alert is now configurable per device (Alerts > Wrist and watch alerts), with short, double, triple, long, insistent and "Call" patterns on Mi Band and Amazfit, which rings until you decline it on the wrist; declining snoozes and silences the phone too. Alerts on Wear OS watches as well. Watch face with glucose and graph on 13 Mi Band/Amazfit models (pick the model on the Mi Band screen) and, new, on Mi Band 8, 9 and 9 Active, which also receive the alerts (not tested with a real band). Sensors: Libre 3/3 Plus and European Libre 2 over Bluetooth, and the MiaoMiao, Bubble and Atom readers (not tested with a real sensor). Integrations: send readings to Dexcom Share (unofficial API, with a warning). Mirror over the internet without a server: tries the reachable address first, then an extra route (STUN). Google Assistant: "glucose" opens GluView and speaks the reading.
