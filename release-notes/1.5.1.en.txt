Settings: a search at the top of the screen (magnifier) finds any setting, including those inside other screens, such as Alerts > IFTTT or Mi Band; tapping a result opens the screen, scrolls to the item and highlights it. Sensor: the "Current sensor" card shows the serial of the sensor in use instead of the region. Charts: the home chart keeps the hours chosen with the pinch gesture and History keeps the period (3, 6, 12 or 24 h) when you leave and come back to the app.
