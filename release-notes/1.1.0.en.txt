New: Wear OS watches in beta (Galaxy Watch 4 or newer, Pixel Watch). Glucose shows on the watch face (complication), in a tile and in the watch app; away from the phone, the watch fetches from the cloud. On Wear OS 6 you can send the watch face you designed in GluView to the watch. The watch app ships with this version (GluView-Wear-1.1.0.apk) and so far has only been tested on emulators. Also: statistics computed on the device when the cloud isn't enabled, fixes for Accu-Chek SmartGuide, watch face preview and file sharing, and GluSync leaves the data source list.
