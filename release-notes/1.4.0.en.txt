Sensors unlocked: every sensor on the list can be chosen by any account, with no beta request. Each one shows whether it "Works", is "In testing" or is "Not tested with a real sensor", and the setup warns before pairing a sensor that has not been validated yet. Only cloud and following still require a request. Dexcom G6 and G7 join the list (not tested with a real sensor): G6 by transmitter ID; G7, already paired by the official app on the same phone, by the 4-digit code, with warm-up, end of life and failures shown as sensor state and recovery of missed readings. AGP report (international CGM standard) in Medical report: hourly percentiles, mean glucose, GMI, variability, time sensor active and time in the 5 ranges, for 7, 14 or 30 days, with a PDF for your doctor. Alerts and Do Not Disturb: "Overrides Do Not Disturb" now sounds as an alarm and can ask for Do Not Disturb access; new per-zone "Vibrates in Do Not Disturb" option; the history shows when Do Not Disturb silenced an alert. Android 16: optional live notification (off by default), with "--" when there is no recent reading. Integrations: send each reading, without the cloud, to apps on the phone that understand the xDrip+ format (e.g. AndroidAPS), only to the apps you select.
