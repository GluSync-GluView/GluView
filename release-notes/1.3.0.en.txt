Health Connect, a watch that logs and a cleaner home screen. In Settings > Health Connect (off by default, each type turns on separately): GluView can write sensor glucose and diary meals, and read exercise, sleep, menstrual cycle, meals, glucose, steps and calories from other apps. Everything stays on the phone and none of it goes through GluSync. The new Patterns screen compares glucose with exercise, sleep and cycle (observation only, not advice). Today's steps and calories show on the glucose card when there is data. On the Wear OS watch: log carbs, insulin (from your list), finger sticks, ketones and exercise by turning the crown, see the day summary and the next reminder, use the "+ Carbs" and "+ Insulin" shortcuts on the tile and the new next-reminder complication; entries reach the phone diary even if the phone is away at the time. Home screen: bigger chart without hour buttons (drag to go back up to 48 h, pinch to change the window, "Now" returns), a more compact glucose card and a 24 h min, average and max row. Report and diary PDFs: choose whether to include the diary and the data cross-check. Widgets that did not load on some phones are fixed. The diary goes to the cloud with the regular cloud access; without it, it stays on the phone. Terms of use updated (version 4).
