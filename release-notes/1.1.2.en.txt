Wear OS watch (beta): away from the phone, the watch now keeps fetching glucose from the cloud for up to 30 days, with its own read-only credential that the phone renews by itself. Before, this lasted about 1 hour. The credential shows up as "Relógio Wear OS" in your key list and can be revoked there. Requires the cloud to be enabled; in local-only mode the watch depends on the phone.
