Full diary and reminders, all on the phone. The diary now logs meals (with food search, servings, favorites and extra carbs), rapid, basal or mixed insulin, finger sticks, ketones (blood or urine, with a caution range), medication, exercise and notes, with a day summary, markers on the chart and a PDF. To log quickly: the pencil on the home screen, two new widgets and the "Log to diary" button on the ongoing notification (you can hide it in Settings > General), which open a quick window without opening the app. Reminders to check glucose, take insulin and take medication ring from the phone itself, with Done (logs the dose), Snooze and the "Basal check" reminder. The diary only goes to GluSync if your account has the cloud enabled; otherwise it stays on the device. Backups now include the diary and reminders. The food database comes from the USDA (public domain) and its values are approximate: check the label.
